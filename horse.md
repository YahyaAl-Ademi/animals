# Amazing horse 

Horses have long necks, long legs, and singular solid hooves on the ends of their feet. Through years of breeding, humans have developed many different colors and patterns in their fur. Some of the most common colors are grey, dark reddish-brown (*known as __bay__*), and light brown (*known as __chestnut__*).
Different breeds range drastically in size. Miniature Horses, the smallest of the breeds, measure under 3 ft. tall at the shoulder, and weigh around 200 lbs. Contrastingly, Shires are the largest breed, standing nearly 6 ft. tall and weighing up to 2,400 lbs. or more.
