# Scary lion 

The lion, often called the __king of the jungle,__ is a powerful and majestic animal known for its strength, courage, and deep, echoing roar. Living mostly in the grasslands of Africa, lions are social cats that live in family groups called prides. With their golden coats and impressive manes, especially in males, they command attention and respect. More than just hunters, lions symbolize leadership and unity — a wild reminder of nature’s balance and beauty.
